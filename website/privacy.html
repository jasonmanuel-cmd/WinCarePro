Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Zero Telemetry & 100% Offline Operation

WinCare Pro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or machine data. There are no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting servers, and no online license-activation checks.

Local Files Stored on Your PC

  • history.json — Local record of actions and reversible prior-state payloads.
  • options.json — Header refresh interval and opt-in local diagnostics preference.
  • license.json — Your offline WCP2. license token when activated.
  • crash.log & environment.txt — Off by default. Created locally only if you opt in under Options.

Checkout & Payment Providers

When you buy a license via our checkout provider or the Microsoft Store, billing details are processed directly by that merchant of record under their privacy policy. WinCare Pro itself never handles payment card data.
