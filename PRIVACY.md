# WinCare Pro Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 2, 2026

WinCare Pro is designed to keep its operational data on the Windows PC where it
runs. It stores settings, audit logs, license state, Guided Care snapshots, and
timeline records under the current user's local application-data folders. This
data is used to operate requested features, show history, support rollback, and
verify approvals. WinCare Pro does not include advertising or product-usage
telemetry and does not sell or share this local data for behavioral advertising.

Network access occurs only for a feature the user invokes or configures:

- License activation sends the entered purchase email and license key to
  Gumroad's HTTPS license-verification service.
- Update checks retrieve the configured HTTPS update manifest; approved updates
  download a signed installer from its configured HTTPS address.
- The optional AI connection talks to a user-run Ollama service on the local PC.
- Windows tools such as `winget` may contact their own configured providers.

Support information is previewed before the user chooses to copy or share it.
WinCare Pro does not transmit the support summary itself. Local records remain
until the user deletes them or removes the application data. Uninstalling the
program may not remove records intentionally retained for audit or rollback.

Users can inspect or delete local WinCare Pro records through the application
and Windows file controls. For privacy questions or rights requests, use the
support contact shown on the purchase receipt or the product checkout page.
Third-party services process data under their own privacy terms.

This notice must be reviewed whenever data collection, providers, or purposes
change. Commercial release also requires the seller to confirm that its public
checkout notice and privacy policy identify the legal seller and applicable
regional rights.
